Who we are

Technology is evolving, and learning how to use digital tools is only the beginning. What matters is the ability to apply those skills, solve real-world problems, and leverage Artificial Intelligence to work smarter and create meaningful solutions.

At Othello Institute of Technology (OIT), we go beyond theoretical lessons. We equip learners with practical technology skills through guided training, hands-on projects, real-world case studies, and exposure to emerging technologies.

Whether you are a beginner looking to start a career in technology or a professional seeking to advance your skills, we provide a supportive learning environment designed to help you grow.

Our vision and mission

Our Vision

To become the number one technology academy in Ojo, Lagos, delivering quality, practical, and industry-relevant technology education that prepares learners for an ever-evolving digital world.

Our Mission

To equip young people, secondary school leavers, undergraduates, graduates, job seekers, professionals, business owners, Corps Members & IT Students and career changers with practical, industry-relevant technology skills through experienced instructors, empowering them to enhance their employability, advance their careers, and succeed in entrepreneurship.

What makes OIT different

Othello Institute of Technology delivers affordable, practical, hands-on technology education complemented by internationally oriented certification opportunities through its Canadian certification partnership.

  • Canadian Accreditation: OIT is accredited by Skill Development Council Canada, and graduates can apply for a Canadian certificate through our partnership.
  • Practical, Hands-on Training: guided exercises, assignments and real-world projects.
  • Affordable and Accessible Education: accessible fees with flexible payment arrangements.

Our three learning pillars

Guided Learning

We break down complex technical concepts into understandable lessons and guide learners throughout their training journey.

Practical Application

Students engage in exercises, assignments and real-world projects that develop their technical abilities and problem-solving skills.

AI Integration

We introduce relevant Artificial Intelligence tools and techniques that help learners improve productivity, work smarter and adapt to the evolving technology landscape.
