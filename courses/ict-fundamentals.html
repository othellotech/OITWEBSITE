About this course

ICT Fundamentals is OIT’s starting point for anyone who wants to become confident and productive with computers. Over 10 weeks you move from how a computer works to using email, the internet and the most widely used office and productivity tools for study, work and everyday life.

The course is designed for beginners, including secondary school leavers, undergraduates, job seekers, business owners and professionals who want to strengthen their digital literacy. You can start from zero.

What you will learn

  • How computers work and how to operate them confidently
  • The difference between computer hardware and software, and what each does
  • Everyday computer use and digital literacy, including staying safe online
  • How to search, browse and navigate the internet effectively
  • How to set up, send and manage email professionally
  • How to create and format documents in Microsoft Word
  • How to design and deliver presentations in Microsoft PowerPoint
  • How to organise and calculate data in Microsoft Excel
  • How to create and collaborate on files in Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Slides

Curriculum and learning modules

The programme runs for 10 weeks. Modules follow a logical sequence, and the weekly breakdown below shows how the topics are organised.

  1. Week 1

    Computers, hardware and software

    • Introduction to computers and computer operations
    • Computer hardware and software fundamentals
    • Using the keyboard and mouse, and managing files and folders
  2. Week 2

    Digital literacy and the internet

    • Everyday computer usage and digital literacy
    • Internet usage and navigation, and searching effectively
    • Staying safe online
  3. Week 3

    Email communication and management

    • Creating an email account and sending, replying and attaching files
    • Organising your inbox
    • Professional email etiquette
  4. Weeks 4–5

    Microsoft Word

    • Typing, text formatting and document layout
    • Headings, lists, tables and images
    • Headers, footers, page setup and printing
    • Writing a CV and a formal letter
  5. Week 6

    Microsoft PowerPoint

    • Slide layouts, themes and design basics
    • Adding text, images and shapes
    • Transitions, animations and presenting
  6. Weeks 7–8

    Microsoft Excel

    • Workbooks, worksheets, rows and columns
    • Entering and formatting data
    • Basic formulas and functions
    • Sorting, filtering and simple charts
  7. Week 9

    Google Workspace

    • Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Slides
    • Saving and sharing files online
    • Collaborating with comments and shared editing
  8. Week 10

    Review and assessment project

    • Bringing the skills together in a final practical project

Practical exercises and projects

Learning is hands-on. You practise each topic through guided exercises and assignments, and apply what you learn in real-world style projects.

  • A professionally formatted CV and cover letter in Word
  • A presentation on a topic of your choice in PowerPoint
  • A budget or records spreadsheet with formulas and a chart in Excel
  • A shared Google Docs or Slides task completed with other learners
  • A final practical project that combines these tools

Learning objectives and expected competencies

By the end of the course, you should be able to:

  • Operate a computer and manage files with confidence
  • Communicate professionally by email
  • Produce well-formatted documents, presentations and spreadsheets
  • Work and collaborate online using Google Workspace
  • Use the internet safely and effectively

Career and business applications

The skills in this course can be applied in several ways, including:

  • Study: assignments, project reports and presentations
  • Office and administrative tasks that depend on Word, Excel, PowerPoint and email
  • Small-business records, price lists, letters and promotional material
  • A foundation for further technology training, such as our Data Analysis and web development courses

We support our learners beyond the classroom through career guidance, CV and LinkedIn optimisation, mentorship, and access to relevant internship and employment opportunities where available. While employment is not guaranteed, we help learners prepare for available opportunities.
