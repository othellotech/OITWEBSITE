About Us

A Technology Academy in Ojo, Lagos Built for Beginners and Career Switchers

Technology is evolving, and learning how to use digital tools is only the beginning. What matters is the ability to apply those skills, solve real-world problems, and leverage Artificial Intelligence to work smarter and create meaningful solutions.

At Othello Institute of Technology (OIT), we go beyond theoretical lessons. We equip learners with practical technology skills through guided training, hands-on projects, real-world case studies, and exposure to emerging technologies.

Whether you are a beginner looking to start a career in technology or a professional seeking to advance your skills, we provide a supportive learning environment designed to help you grow.

In-Demand Tech Skills to Learn in 2026

Data analysis, software development, cybersecurity, data science, generative AI and UI/UX design are among the most sought-after skills. Not sure where to start? Data analysis training, web development training and ICT fundamentals are good places to begin.
