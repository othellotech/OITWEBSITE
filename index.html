Who We Are

Our Vision, Mission and Core Values

Othello Institute of Technology delivers affordable, practical, hands-on technology education, complemented by internationally oriented certification opportunities through our Canadian certification partnership.

Our Vision

To become the number one technology academy in Ojo, Lagos, delivering quality, practical, and industry-relevant technology education that prepares learners for an ever-evolving digital world.

Our Mission

To equip young people, graduates, job seekers, professionals, business owners, Corps Members & IT Students and career changers with practical, industry-relevant technology skills through experienced instructors, empowering them to enhance their employability, advance their careers, and succeed in entrepreneurship.

Our Core Values

  • Excellence: We keep high standards in teaching, service, learning materials and student experience.
  • Practical Learning: We prioritise hands-on practice, applied assignments and real projects.
  • Integrity: We are honest about course outcomes, fees, certificates and policies.
  • Student Success: We support learners towards employment, career growth or entrepreneurship.
  • Professionalism: We communicate respectfully, meet commitments and stay organised.
  • Discipline: We are punctual, follow agreed processes and uphold fair standards.
  • Continuous Learning: We keep improving our teaching, course content and systems.
